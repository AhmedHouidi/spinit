Questions about spinning the wheel

What is Whirl?

Whirl is a free online spinning wheel. Spin it to pick a random color, a random number from any range, or one item from your own list of names, foods, or ideas.

How do I pick a random color?

Open the Colors tab, choose a palette and how many colors you want, then press Spin. The wheel lands on one color and shows its hex code and closest name.

How do I pick a random number?

Open the Numbers tab, set the From and To values, and press Spin. For big ranges the wheel shows a random sample of numbers.

Can I make a wheel with my own names or choices?

Yes. In the Anything tab, type one item per line. You can save wheels in your browser and load them later.

How do weighted odds work?

Add x and a number after an item, like Pizza x3. Its slice becomes three times bigger and three times as likely to win.

Is the wheel really random?

The Spin buttons use your browser’s secure random number generator. The fairness test runs 1,000 spins so you can compare results to the expected odds. If you flick the wheel by hand, the result depends on your flick.

Is Whirl free, and is my data private?

Whirl is free. Your lists, saved wheels, and history stay in your browser and are not uploaded.

Privacy and ads

Your lists, saved wheels and spin history stay in your browser. Whirl never uploads them.
